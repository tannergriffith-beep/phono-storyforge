# 08 — Open Questions

Unknowns, assumptions, missing research, and areas needing clarification before or during a redesign. Grouped by area.

## Product scope

- **Foundation (K–2) and Independence (6–8) unit structures are undecided.** Source explicitly says "still planning, need decision." Will they mirror the Fluency units (Decoding / Word Study / Fluency / Comprehension) or differ by grade band?
- **Summer toolkit scope not fully locked.** Research is active; confirm the dual-track (Younger K–2 / Older 3–8) model and final tool list.
- **App scope is conceptual.** The interactive session app and AI tutoring agent are envisioned but not specified in depth (platform, offline behavior, data model, safety framework).
- **Video library vs. interactive app** — which form the "show, don't tell" demonstration content takes is undecided.

## Customers & research

- **Persona file is partly a placeholder and the feedback log is empty.** No real customer data yet — the business is pre-launch. Personas here are hypotheses needing validation.
- **Buyer split unknown:** How much of the audience is parents vs. educators? If educators dominate, the revenue model may need rethinking.
- **No validated acquisition or conversion data:** assumed email-capture and email→sale conversion rates are untested.

## Business & pricing

- **Pricing is inconsistent across documents** — a lean frame (~$24 Summer / ~$37 Fluency, one-time) vs. an earlier tiered frame ($29 starter / $69 library / $12-mo subscription). **Needs a single decision.**
- **Subscription vs. one-time** is unresolved for future phases.
- **Specialist directory monetization** (listing fees? referral fees? loss leader?) is undecided.
- **Revenue targets stated as both "$10k–$25k" and "$25k validate / 1,000 units stretch"** — reconcile the numbers.
- **Co-ownership / entity questions** (possible second owner, equity, IP contribution) are open but largely operational, not design-relevant.

## Go-to-market

- **Platform-concentration risk:** the growth plan leans almost entirely on organic short-form social with no paid spend and a thin secondary-channel contingency. No clear fallback if the algorithm shifts.
- **Breakout-reach assumptions** are acknowledged as upside, not base case — don't design around virality.
- **Collaboration sourcing** (educators/specialists) is named but the mechanism is undefined.

## Accessibility & technical (most relevant to a redesign)

- **No stated accessibility conformance target** (e.g., WCAG 2.1 AA). Recommend the agency propose and confirm one.
- **No performance budgets** for web/app surfaces, and no defined offline requirement for the app.
- **Text-to-speech / audio support** is implied by the methodology but not specified as a built feature — clarify whether the product provides audio or relies on third-party audiobooks.
- **Print constraints** (paper size, color vs. grayscale printing, page counts) for the printable products are not formally documented for a fresh design.
- **Localization:** English/US-only assumed; future internationalization undecided — confirm whether to design for eventual multi-language.

## Compliance & legal

- **COPPA / child-data handling** for future child-facing software needs a concrete consent and data-retention plan.
- **Claims review:** the line between "reading support/screening" and medical/diagnostic claims needs legal sign-off; affects copy in any redesign.
- **IP assignment** must be in place for any external design contributor.

## Assumptions a redesigner should make explicit and verify

1. The **specialist-first, honest** positioning is permanent and central — do not design anything that undercuts it (e.g., over-promising outcomes).
2. The **parent is the buyer; the child is the end user** — both must be served, with different reading levels and emotional needs.
3. **Short, low-pressure, shame-free** experiences are non-negotiable for the child-facing parts.
4. **Print is the current primary medium**; web and app are future phases — confirm what's in scope for this engagement.
5. The product must remain **credibly evidence-aligned** (IDA / structured literacy / Orton-Gillingham); design should reinforce, not dilute, that credibility.
